The artist

My art story

All I have ever really wanted to be is an artist.

So many things — and so many years — stood in my way. But in 2017, I finally felt brave enough to grab hold of my dream and make it happen. Art is so much more to me than simply creating.

Every stroke of a brush and every line from my pencil speaks of my journey.

More than that, it is a testimony to God giving me the desire of my heart. In my artist's heart, I can see Him cheering me on, encouraging me, and gently pushing me beyond my own limits so that I can create on the level He has set out for me.

No two artists create from the same place. Just like the quote by Paul Avellino says, no one sees the rainbow in exactly the same way. The angle, the light, the colours — they are all shaped by where we stand. The same is true of art.

No one else can create from my perspective, my experiences, my journey, or my faith. And perhaps that is what makes art so beautiful — every piece carries a little of the artist's soul and a glimpse of the unique way they see the world.

What to expect

  • Beginners welcome. Irene starts at the very beginning and goes at the group's pace. If you have never held a brush, this is for you.
  • Small groups. A handful of people at a time, so there is room for your questions and a hand when you need one.
  • Materials made simple. When you book, Irene tells you what to bring and what will be waiting for you on the day.
